Operating area map

Upload a map showing the actual geographic area where the aircraft will operate.

How to prepare the map

Do not assume a facility's mailing address is the correct GPS location for the operation. Large sites such as stadiums, race tracks, shopping centers, campuses, construction sites, farms, and industrial facilities may cover a large geographic area. The address pin may be at an entrance, office, or other point far from the actual flight area.

  1. Open Google Maps, Google Earth, or another current satellite mapping service.
  2. Zoom to the exact part of the property where flight operations will occur.
  3. In Google Maps, click or right-click the actual operating area to obtain its latitude and longitude. Use those coordinates for the Operation Latitude / Longitude fields.
  4. Separately identify the exact launch / recovery point when it is different from the center of operations.
  5. Save or capture a map and annotate it before uploading.

The uploaded map should identify

  • Planned UAS operating area or boundary.
  • Launch and normal recovery location.
  • Any no-flight or excluded areas.
  • Emergency landing area(s), when applicable.
  • Major roads, buildings, property boundaries, or other visual references useful to the RPIC and FAA reviewer.

Example: at a large facility or shopping center, the facility address may place the map pin hundreds or thousands of feet from the intended flight area. Select the actual operating area on the map and use that GPS coordinate instead.
